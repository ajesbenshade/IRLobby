The Foyer is where your church family plans time together outside Sunday morning.

See the gatherings coming up at your church, say you're going, and bring your family along. Host a potluck, a game night, a work day or a walk, and choose who it's for.

• Upcoming gatherings from your church, with the place and time
• RSVP for yourself and the people in your family
• Host a gathering in a few taps, and approve guests if you want to
• Chat with the people going to a gathering
• Share photos from a gathering with everyone who was there
• Friends, and simple privacy controls for what others can see

The Foyer is being tested with a few Mennonite churches right now.
