The Foyer: Terms of Use

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Welcome to The Foyer ("the Foyer," "the app"). It is a free app that helps members and friends of local Mennonite churches find and host small gatherings.

The Foyer is run by Aaron Esbenshade ("we," "us"). Churches listed in the app take part, but they do not run the app. By making an account or using the app, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Questions? Write to us at support@irlobby.com.

1. Who can use the Foyer

  • You must be at least 13 years old to have your own account.
  • Children under 13 cannot have an account. A parent or guardian may add them as a family member on the parent's own account. We ask for the family member's name and full birth date. We use the birth date only to check event age ranges.
  • If you are under 18, some features are limited to keep you safer (for example, messages are friends-only).
  • Please give us true information and keep your login private. You are responsible for what happens on your account.

2. The app is free

The Foyer is free. There are no ads, no payments, and no donations in the app.

3. Gatherings: church-hosted and member-hosted

There are two kinds of gatherings:

  • Church-hosted events are put on by a church listed in the app.
  • Member-hosted gatherings are put on by individual members or friends of a church.

Please understand this clearly:

  • Hosts of member-hosted gatherings are members, not employees or agents of the church. The church does not run or supervise these gatherings.
  • Gatherings happen in person. Please use your own good judgment about whether to host or attend, and about who you are with.
  • The church and the operator are not responsible for what happens at gatherings, or for what other people say or do. Nothing here limits responsibilities the law says cannot be limited.
  • Hosts can cancel a gathering. If they do, people who said they were coming get a push notification.
  • Some hosts ask for approval before you can join. See our Privacy Policy for what a host can see when you ask to join.
  • Private addresses on member-hosted gatherings are shown only to guests who are approved or going.

4. Being kind and safe: what is not allowed

Please treat each other as neighbors. You may not use the Foyer to:

  • harass, bully, threaten, or demean anyone;
  • share hateful content about people or groups;
  • post sexual content;
  • pretend to be someone else (impersonation);
  • do anything illegal;
  • send spam or unwanted promotions;
  • share other people's private information (such as addresses, phone numbers, or photos) without their OK;
  • share unsafe or adult-only content around minors, or host anything unsafe for the children who may be present.

5. Your content

You keep ownership of what you post (photos, bio, messages, gathering details). By posting, you let us show it inside the app to the people you chose, so the app can work. That is all the permission we need.

  • Only post photos and details you have the right to share.
  • Photos can be downloaded. Other people who attended the same gathering may be able to download photos from that gathering. Downloads use temporary signed links. Once someone saves a photo, we can't take it back from their phone. Please post only what you are comfortable sharing with the people at that gathering.

6. Reporting, blocking, and moderation

  • You can report and block from profiles, chat messages, and photos.
  • Blocking works both ways: you won't see them, and they won't see you.
  • Church administrators can review reports. They may remove content, limit features, or remove accounts that break these terms or put people at risk. We may do this without advance notice when safety is at stake.
  • If you think someone is in danger, contact local emergency services first.

7. Your account

You can delete your account any time in the app: Settings > Account > Delete my account. This removes your profile and contact info, friends, chats, photos you uploaded, family members, RSVPs, and your own messages in gathering chats. Gatherings you host are deleted: upcoming ones are cancelled first, and attendees are told. We keep a minimal record of safety reports for up to 12 months so we can protect other members. Backups are cleared within 30 days. The Privacy Policy has the full list.

We may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms.
